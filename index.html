About Pam

Pamela Cochrane Therapy was founded in 2001 and has been providing innovative treatments based on proven technology and products for two and a half decades. With SRN qualifications and beauty therapist status, Pamela Cochrane does the treatments herself, and is able to provide treatments that are out of the range of ordinary beauty salons.

Treatments are results driven and a large contingent of clients can attest to the benefits of Pam Cochrane’s treatments, being either a smoother face or more contoured, slimmer, healthier looking body. With decades of experience Pam herself tailors treatment protocols and products to a client’s individual requests or requirements.

Pam continually selects new technology for treatments and products that provide results with cost effectiveness in mind. Clients can book for a schedule of targeted treatments, or just for a single treatment for maintenance purposes.
